About this shop

Maverik Electric Inc is family-owned by Matt Meyer and his wife Meghan. Matt started out in the new home building and remodeling market.

Later, he went on to complete his apprenticeship and earned his electricians license in California. After graduating, he spent 10 years working for the same large electrical contractor in San Diego County as a journeyman and foreman.

Maverik Electric Inc. was founded in June 2020 as a full-service electric company. We provide state-of-the-art electrician services for residential and commercial buildings and specialize in low-voltage electrical services. We diagnose and repair issues professionally and efficiently.

We’re a woman-owned business with a strong set of values and standards for quality. We hire the most experienced electricians. From there, they go through our specialized training program. We prepare our electricians for every possible outcome to get the job done right the first time.

When you are looking for a reliable electrician to handle ALL your electrical needs with integrity and at a fair price, you’ve come to the right place. We take our work seriously and treat every client with respect above all else. Our highly skilled technician is committed to earning your trust by completing your job on time, on budget, and to the highest degree of safety.

Are you looking for electrical work in San Diego? We have highly trained and experienced electricians on staff who diagnose and repair your issues in a timely fashion. Let us know if you’re having trouble with your breaker, wiring, or appliances. We’ll make sure your wiring is up to par.
